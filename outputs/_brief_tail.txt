### Scenario C — Photo series (drive-by / approach)\n- **Reconstruction:** hloc (DISK+LightGlue) matching → COLMAP CPU sparse SfM (sequential matcher, overlap ~10, ~1000 feats/frame, ratio 0.7, ≥30 inliers) → OpenMVS CPU dense cloud.\n- **Scale:** reference object in frame (manhole 600 mm clear opening; or a known marking width); set the model's metric scale from two tie-points of known real distance. GPS/EXIF (OpenSfM-style) is only meter-level — coarse prior, never for cm depth.\n- **Depth + volume:** in CloudCompare — segment surrounding pavement, RANSAC-fit reference plane, `Compute 2.5D Volume` (plane = ground/before, pothole surface = ceil/after); **depth = max perpendicular distance from plane to pit floor.** This is the **only scenario that yields trustworthy cm depth and volume.**\n- **Confidence:** **high** for length/width/area; **medium-high** for depth/volume. Peer-reviewed best case ~**1% volume error** (controlled 90-photo arc capture) — real CPU drive-by with fewer/oblique frames will be worse; treat 1% as a ceiling, not a guarantee. (https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10347019/)\n- **Capture protocol (bake into the app's guidance):** camera must **translate** (pure rotation breaks SfM); ~20–60 frames, 70–80% overlap; slow the vehicle; capture **oblique looks into the cavity** + multiple heights so floor/walls are seen; keep a known reference (manhole/marking) in several frames.\n\n**Confidence summary**\n\n| Quantity | A (no ref) | B (ref) | C (series) |\n|---|---|---|---|\n| Shape ratios (eccentricity, solidity) | med-high | high | high |\n| Diameter / width / length / area (cm) | not reported | **±10–20%** | **high** |\n| Depth (cm) | not reported | low (±1–3 cm, indicative) | **med-high** |\n| Volume | — | — | med-high (~1–5%+) |\n\n---\n\n## 3. Reference-Object Scaling — GOST Numbers to Bake In\n\n**Scale anchors**\n- **ГОСТ 3634** round manhole — **clear opening (лаз) = 600 mm** → *primary scale anchor*; standardized and identical across light Л(А15) and heavy Т(С250), confirmed by the \"-60\" suffix. Cover (крышка) outer ≈ **646 mm**; supporting base/frame ≈ **750–840 mm** by class (verify per edition 89/99/2019 — only 600 mm is stable; some 3634-89 variants used 550 mm). Detect the top circle; decide explicitly whether you measure the 600 mm opening or the 646 mm lid rim. (https://docs.cntd.ru/document/1200006883)\n- **ГОСТ Р 51256-2018** road markings — *secondary/corroborating* (category-dependent, tolerance ±0.01 m):\n  - Narrow longitudinal line **1.1 = 0.10 m** (0.08 m cat IV; 0.15 m cat IA restricted-zone)\n  - Edge line **1.2/1.2.1 = 0.10–0.20 m** (0.20 cat IA / 0.15 cat IБ / 0.10 cat II–III)\n  - **STOP-line 1.12 = 0.40 m** wide. (https://docs.cntd.ru/document/1200158480)\n\n**Severity thresholds — ГОСТ Р 50597-2017** (clause 5.2.4 / Table 5.3; supersedes the legacy \"15×60×5 cm\" of 50597-93):\n- Single defect (выбоина/просадка/пролом) is **non-conforming when length ≥ 15 cm AND depth ≥ 5 cm AND area ≥ 0.06 m²** (all three).\n- **Repair deadlines (individual defects):** cat IA = 1 day; IБ/IВ = 3; II = 5; III = 7; IV = 10; V = 12 days.\n- **Aggregated small defects per 100 m lane:** > 0.1 m²/100 m (IA–IВ, 5 d) … up to > 5.2 m²/100 m (V, 20 d); II = 7, III = 10, IV = 14 d.\n- **Clause 4.4:** hazardous out-of-spec section must be signed and, if needed, fenced **within 2 hours** of detection.\n(https://legalacts.ru/doc/gost-r-50597-2017-natsionalnyi-standart-rossiiskoi-federatsii-dorogi-avtomobilnye/)\n\n**Pass/fail implication for the engine:** length and area are decidable in Scenario B/C; the **depth ≥ 5 cm** leg is only decidable in **Scenario C** (or with a sensor). In A/B, report length/area against GOST and mark depth as \"not certifiable — series capture required.\"\n\n---\n\n## 4. CPU / Windows Feasibility — Risks & Mitigations\n\n**Feasible on CPU-only Windows (per-image, ONNX/OpenVINO):**\n- Detector (YOLOv12s) ~100–180 ms; MobileSAM encoder ~1–4 s/img + ~tens ms/box; Depth Anything V2 Small ~75–150 ms (OpenVINO).
